Cell Selection
  1. Single: Click any cell
  2. Multi: Ctrl/Cmd + click
  3. Range drag: Click and drag
  4. Range shift: Click, then Shift+click
  5. Right-click: Context menu
  6. Double-click: Edit cell
  7. Row / column: Click a ruler number or letter
